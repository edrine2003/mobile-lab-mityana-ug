About Us

Mobile Lab Mityana is a digital laboratory service designed to make medical testing easier, faster, and more accessible. We allow patients to order laboratory tests online, receive results digitally, and communicate directly with laboratory professionals — all from their phones.

🎯
Our Mission

To provide reliable, fast, and accessible laboratory diagnostic services using modern technology, while maintaining the highest standards of medical professionalism.

🔭
Our Vision

To become a trusted digital laboratory platform that connects patients and healthcare providers through efficient, innovative diagnostic solutions across Uganda.

🔬
Services We Offer
  • Complete Blood Count (CBC) & Blood Tests
  • HIV, Hepatitis B & C Testing
  • Malaria (Blood Slide) Testing
  • Urine & Stool Analysis
  • Women's Health Tests (HVS, HCG)
  • STI & Infection Screening
  • Home Sample Collection
  • Digital Result Delivery
  • Doctor Chat & Consultation
